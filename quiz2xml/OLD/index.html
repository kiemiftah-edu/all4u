Time Saving Strategy:
No need to arrange answers manually.
• Write all correct answers at Option A.
• Ensure 'Shuffle Answers' is ON in settings.
• Moodle will randomize the order automatically.
Flexible Format:
Use English or Malay keywords:
- QUESTION: or SOALAN:
- CORRECT_ANSWER: or JAWAPAN:
- MARK: or MARKAH:
